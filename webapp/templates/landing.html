How it works

STEP 01

Upload marks + notes

A report-card/marks CSV across terms, plus teacher remarks or syllabus notes. Add interests and any learning differences a tutor should know about.

STEP 02

We learn their learning patterns

Not tone or vocabulary - academic level, specific knowledge gaps, recurring mistake patterns, pace and style. Every tutoring session logs structured progress so trends (and regressions) show up, not just a one-time snapshot.

STEP 03

Connect, anywhere

One private link. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP-compatible AI and tutoring is adapted to this student from the first message, in every chat they open.

What you actually get

Patterns, not just topics

"Weak at fractions" isn't that useful on its own. We track HOW mistakes happen - rushes under pressure, skips re-reading questions - so the tutor can head off the next mistake, not just correct the last one.

It keeps growing - and can regress

Every topic has a real trend (struggling → practicing → mastered, or back again) logged from actual tutoring sessions, not a single static label from the day you uploaded a report card.

Works where they already study

No new app to learn, no walled-garden platform. It shows up as part of the AI workspace the student already uses for homework help.

Private by default

Raw report cards and notes never leave this server - only the derived learner profile and progress updates are ever shared with a connected AI. The link is a private, unguessable URL. Delete a student any time, permanently.
