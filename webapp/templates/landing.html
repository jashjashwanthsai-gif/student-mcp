The gap
this closes

A parent, tutor, or teacher can see the pattern - "she rushes through word problems," "he's fine until fractions show up." A fresh AI chat can't. It gives the same explanation it would give anyone, finds out the hard way what doesn't land, and forgets all of it by the next conversation.

Who this is for

Built around however you actually support a student, not one specific role.

Parents

You already know your kid's weak spots better than any placement test. Hand that knowledge to whatever AI they use for homework, instead of repeating it over their shoulder every night.

Private tutors

Running several students across different subjects, you don't have time to re-explain each one's history at the start of every session. Set it up once per student, done.

Tutoring centers & pods

Each student gets their own private profile and link, so staff can hand off a student without losing a term's worth of context - and no one's notes get mixed up with anyone else's.

Homeschool families

Managing multiple kids across multiple subjects, with no separate teacher to track each one's quirks - this keeps that tracking in one place, per child.

Teachers

For the student who needs more practice than class time allows, give them AI help after hours that's calibrated to where they're actually stuck, not generic grade-level review.

What actually changes

Patterns, not just topics

"Weak at fractions" doesn't tell you much. Knowing a student rushes under time pressure or skips re-reading word problems does - a tutor can watch for that and head it off before it happens again, in any subject.

A trend, not a snapshot

A topic can go struggling → practicing → mastered, or regress after it looked solid - that whole history is what the AI sees, not just what was true the day a report card was uploaded.

Lives where they already study

No separate app to open, no account for the student to remember. It shows up inside the same AI chat they'd already go to for homework help.

Private by default

Raw report cards and notes never leave this platform - only the profile and progress derived from them reach a connected AI. Each link is unguessable, and deleting a student removes everything, permanently.
